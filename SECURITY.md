# Security policy

**Status:** research repository; no production network or customer funds.

Please avoid publishing exploit details or sensitive credentials in public issues. Contact repository maintainers privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature **if enabled**, or request a private reporting channel without sharing exploit details publicly.

In-scope concerns include unsafe transaction-status claims, replay/duplicate handling, compromised relays, dependency compromise, and eventual signing-key risks. Do not test against third-party networks or equipment without authorization. No bug bounty or response-time guarantee is currently offered.
